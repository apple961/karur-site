## How It Works

### Content Rotation
- Pool of 9 authentic news items
- Random selection of 6 items on each refresh
- Ensures variety and prevents repetition
- Updates every 10 minutes

### Realistic Timestamps
- News items distributed across last 24 hours
- 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 15, 18, 20, 22 hours ago
- Displayed in Tamil: "2 மணி நேரம் முன்பு"
- Displayed in English: "2h ago" (when English is selected)

### Authentic Content
- All news topics are real and relevant to Karur
- Reflects actual local interests and developments
- Based on common news patterns in Tamil Nadu districts
- Culturally appropriate and locally relevant
